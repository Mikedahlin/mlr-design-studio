# MLR Website Approval Boundary — Authoritative

Updated: 2026-08-16 02:27 Central

## Approved direction

The homepage is the **only approved visual and interaction direction**, and it is still in active production. Approved homepage elements include the cinematic opening direction, living MLR sign, six-project wheel, pure-black environment, cinematic index direction, and sign-matched controls. Opening cleanup and six replacement wheel films remain unfinished.

## Rejected as final design

Every other existing public page and project page is rejected as a final portfolio design:

- Services
- Work/archive
- About/Studio
- Contact/project inquiry
- White Pine Dental
- Velvet Room
- Apex Motor Co.
- Existing source-preview routes
- Any legacy MLR Assets layout, header, footer, section system, card system, typography system, or generic agency structure

These routes may remain temporarily available only as **prototype/source material**. They are not approved pages to polish, reskin, or treat as production-quality portfolio work. Reusable code, truthful copy, assets, and functional logic may be extracted, but current compositions and architectures are not authoritative.

## Rebuild mandate

The entire website beyond the homepage will be rebuilt around the quality, restraint, cinematic character, accessibility, and originality established by the approved homepage.

Project sites must follow their locked differentiated systems:

- White Pine: calm linear guided-care journey, education, insurance/new-patient flow.
- Velvet Room: nonlinear editorial lookbook, horizontal/magazine layouts, draggable portfolio, moodboard consultation, compact booking drawer.
- Apex: technical build workstation, platform entry, persistent spec, dependency graph, staged builds, data/logging, exploded views/hotspots.
- Iron North: construction/jobsite system, map/archive, capabilities, materials, bid/qualification, timeline/documentation.
- Northshore: spatial property map, accommodations, itinerary, seasons/weather, availability.
- Ember: sensory menu/ingredient/heat, reservation, private events, sourcing, dietary/accessibility filters.

## Production order

1. Finish the homepage opening/sign/index/wheel behavior.
2. Establish the final visual identity and site architecture for one benchmark project.
3. Produce its wheel film from that final direction—not from a rejected prototype page.
4. Approve the benchmark quality bar.
5. Rebuild the remaining five project experiences as genuinely distinct sites and produce their films.
6. Rebuild Work, Services, Studio/About, and Contact around the approved MLR system.
7. Complete accessibility, performance, SEO, truthfulness, responsive QA, and deployment.

## Non-negotiable interpretation

Do not describe any non-home page as finished or approved. Do not spend production time cosmetically polishing rejected layouts. Do not use a rejected project prototype as the visual foundation of a new cinematic film. Preserve useful source material, but redesign the experience.
