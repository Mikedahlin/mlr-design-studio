# Six Wheel Films — Production Roadmap

Updated: 2026-08-16

## Shared delivery specification

- Six project-specific, silent, seamless 10-second films.
- 1280×720 H.264 masters with matching lightweight WebM only after picture approval.
- Strong readable material/action at phone-card scale; no tiny UI copy as the primary image.
- Stable project identity, no generated lettering, no fake testimonials or outcomes.
- First and final frames designed for a clean loop.
- Homepage plays only the center and immediate neighboring cards; distant cards hold posters.

## Benchmark first: White Pine Dental

White Pine has the strongest existing asset kit and a real interactive prototype. It is the right benchmark for proving a better film without inventing a business or relying on weak footage.

### Ten-second shot plan

1. **0.0–2.0 — Material calm:** macro porcelain/white-pine surfaces in soft natural light; restrained parallax, no generic blue clinic imagery.
2. **2.0–4.2 — Guided care:** the curved treatment-path motif forms physically from light and material, then resolves into the site’s calm linear journey.
3. **4.2–6.8 — Useful interaction:** a deliberate new-patient/education interaction using large readable interface regions, not a whole-page screen recording.
4. **6.8–8.8 — Human reassurance:** use the existing illustrative consultation/education imagery with slow controlled camera depth; no testimonial implication.
5. **8.8–10.0 — Loop handoff:** return through white material/light to the opening macro surface and lock the White Pine mark/visual motif without generated text.

## Remaining films

### Velvet Room
Black velvet and liquid pigment → nonlinear editorial lookbook → draggable portfolio moment → consultation moodboard → pigment closes the loop. Avoid generic salon slideshow structure.

### Apex Motor Co.
Oily black metal/reflection → technical component inspection → platform/spec workstation → dependency/data moment → exploded mechanical silhouette → reflection returns to black. Preserve approved original film until this benchmark is demonstrably stronger; never invent power figures.

### Iron North
Cold steel/concrete/jobsite textures → survey/grid → project map/archive → material/capability assembly → documentation/timeline → welding spark closes into first frame. Construction concept remains distinct from Majestic Pine.

### Northshore Lodge
Lake mist/water surface → spatial property map → accommodation reveal → itinerary/season transition → availability gesture → mist closes the loop. Quiet, tactile, no generic hotel booking montage.

### Ember
Charcoal/ingredient macro → heat reveals menu structure → sourcing/dietary filter → reservation/private-event gesture → ember field returns to opening coal. Premium hospitality/dining; never use “Supper Club” framing in new copy.

## Approval gate

Produce and review the White Pine benchmark first. Once its material-to-interface balance, readability, loop, and compression are approved, apply the same quality bar—not the same composition—to the other five.
