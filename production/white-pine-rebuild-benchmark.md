# White Pine Dental — Rebuild Benchmark Brief

Status: isolated replacement prototype; current public page remains rejected.

## Core idea
A single guided-care line that moves from concern to understanding, visit preparation, insurance/cost literacy, education, and appointment request. The experience avoids a generic hero followed by interchangeable cards.

## Conversion path
1. Name the concern in human terms.
2. Understand what a visit may include.
3. Prepare practical information and comfort preferences.
4. Learn what to ask about insurance and estimates.
5. Carry the selected concern into a short request flow.

## Truthfulness
Fictional concept practice. No clinicians, testimonials, outcomes, claims, real availability, diagnosis, or submitted health data. Emergency and medical guidance remain outside the interactive demo.

## Wheel film consequence
The eventual film should visualize the guided line and concern-to-clarity transformation from this final direction—not animate screenshots of the rejected prototype.
