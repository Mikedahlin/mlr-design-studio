# Six Wheel Films — Cinematic Interaction Bible

Updated: 2026-08-16 03:11 Central

## Shared rule

Each silent ten-second film transforms a project-specific physical material into that project’s defining interaction, then lands on an exact HTML entry frame. Generated media supplies environments, material motion, and camera movement only. Logos, interface copy, data, diagrams, and claims are manually constructed and composited. No malformed generated text, fake outcomes, fake people, fake availability, or invented performance figures.

---

# 1. White Pine Dental — Three Paths to Clarity

**Defining interaction:** insurance, availability, and cost answers carried into a guided-care request.

- **0.0–1.8:** Macro pale-pine grain and translucent porcelain in morning light. Three faint illuminated paths form beneath the surface.
- **1.8–3.5:** Paths separate into coverage, timing, and estimate objects. No insurer branding or generic floating icons.
- **3.5–5.3:** Coverage path becomes the real manually composited plan-check field. “Sample PPO” resolves to “Verification before booking,” never “accepted.”
- **5.3–7.0:** Timing path separates routine/new-patient from pain/specific-concern routing. No fake appointment dates.
- **7.0–8.5:** Cost path illuminates VISIT → BENEFITS → ESTIMATE with no invented dollar amount.
- **8.5–10.0:** Paths join into the fixed White Pine tree/smile mark and land on the exact rebuilt HTML hero frame. Grain/light returns to frame zero for the wheel loop.

**Open-site handoff:** wheel card expands; final video frame holds; identical live hero appears underneath; video fades; guided-care interaction is immediately usable.

# 2. Velvet Room — The Editorial Cut

**Defining interaction:** nonlinear lookbook, draggable portfolio, visual moodboard consultation, compact booking drawer.

- **0.0–1.5:** Macro black velvet under a narrow moving light. A drop of oxblood/magenta pigment lands and travels through the fibers.
- **1.5–3.2:** Pigment cuts the frame into irregular editorial panels—portrait crop, texture, silhouette, detail—like physical magazine sheets sliding over velvet.
- **3.2–5.0:** A handless directional gesture drags the image strip laterally. Panels move at different depths; this previews the actual draggable lookbook rather than scrolling a page.
- **5.0–6.8:** Three selected images peel from the strip and pin themselves to a consultation moodboard. Exact manual labels appear only here: SHAPE / TEXTURE / TONE.
- **6.8–8.3:** The moodboard compresses into a narrow booking drawer at the right edge. Selected direction carries into the drawer; no fake stylist or available time appears.
- **8.3–10.0:** Drawer closes like a velvet fold. Its seam becomes the fixed folded-V mark and lands on the exact live editorial entrance. Pigment withdraws through the same fiber path to loop.

**Open-site handoff:** the final magazine panel remains under the pointer and becomes draggable HTML with no layout jump.

**Avoid:** salon montage, before/after gimmicks, generic appointment calendar, pink beauty-template styling.

# 3. Apex Motor Co. — Dependency Ignition

**Defining interaction:** build workstation with persistent vehicle spec, dependency graph, staged build logic, diagnostics, and exploded technical views.

- **0.0–1.6:** Black automotive paint in darkness. A controlled strip reflection reveals one body line, then travels into machined metal.
- **1.6–3.2:** A component—brake, cooling, fuel, or suspension element—separates into a clean exploded view. Parts remain mechanically credible; no fantasy engine internals.
- **3.2–4.9:** Component nodes connect into the actual dependency graph: intended use → tires/brakes/cooling/supporting systems. Exact labels are composited manually.
- **4.9–6.5:** The graph warns that one power choice affects heat, fuel, drivetrain, diagnostics, reliability, and legal/emissions considerations. No horsepower number appears.
- **6.5–8.1:** Three staged paths snap into place: DAILY / STREET–TRACK / CLOSED COURSE. The persistent spec rail remains visible across the choice.
- **8.1–10.0:** Exploded parts reassemble with one precise mechanical lock. The final reflected body line becomes the live workstation entry frame, then sweeps back to opening black paint for the loop.

**Open-site handoff:** final film graph aligns with live SVG/HTML graph; pointer can immediately inspect a dependency node.

**Avoid:** speedometer clichés, invented power gains, random supercar footage, configuration choices with no supporting-system consequences.

# 4. Iron North — From Site to Record

**Defining interaction:** construction/jobsite system with project map/archive, capabilities, materials, qualification, timeline, and documentation.

- **0.0–1.6:** Black frame struck by a welding arc. Sparks reveal raw steel plate, concrete dust, chalk marks, and a survey point.
- **1.6–3.2:** Chalk/survey lines extend across the plate and become a spatial project map. Three project pins rise like physical datum markers.
- **3.2–4.8:** Selecting one pin opens a vertical cut through the project timeline: SITE → STRUCTURE → ENCLOSURE → FINISH → RECORD. No fake client claim or result.
- **4.8–6.5:** Material samples—steel, timber, concrete, envelope—slot into capability positions while document stamps attach to the timeline.
- **6.5–8.2:** The qualification path asks project type, location, stage, and documentation available. It does not promise a bid or invent project capacity.
- **8.2–10.0:** Timeline compresses into one heavy black project ledger. Its steel edge becomes the exact live archive entrance; a welding spark travels backward to frame zero for the loop.

**Open-site handoff:** final map and selected project pin become live; keyboard/pointer can move through location, phase, materials, and documents.

**Avoid:** generic worker montage, fake finished projects, fitness/gym language, replacing Iron North with Majestic Pine.

# 5. Northshore Lodge — The Stay Takes Shape

**Defining interaction:** spatial property map connected to accommodations, itinerary, season/weather context, and truthful availability inquiry.

- **0.0–1.7:** Dark lake water before dawn. Mist moves laterally; one cedar roofline appears as a reflection before the building itself is visible.
- **1.7–3.4:** The reflection becomes a topographic property map etched into wet stone. Lodge, cabins, dock, trail, and gathering spaces illuminate spatially.
- **3.4–5.1:** Camera chooses one accommodation pin; the map rises into a sectional room/environment view with capacity and amenity categories—no invented rating or guest claim.
- **5.1–6.8:** A day arc moves across the map. Itinerary nodes connect MORNING / AFTERNOON / EVENING and visibly adjust by season.
- **6.8–8.3:** Weather/season layer changes mist, water, trail, and indoor emphasis. Availability is represented as “check dates/request” unless connected to a real inventory source.
- **8.3–10.0:** Topography settles back into the lake reflection and lands on the exact live property map entrance. Mist returns to the opening position for the loop.

**Open-site handoff:** video’s selected map pin becomes the currently focused live accommodation; map remains pannable and keyboard accessible.

**Avoid:** generic luxury-hotel montage, fake room inventory, fake reviews, disconnected booking widget.

# 6. Ember — Heat Finds the Table

**Defining interaction:** sensory ingredient/heat menu connected to sourcing, dietary/accessibility filters, reservation, and private events.

- **0.0–1.5:** One coal breathes in pure black. Heat reveals ingredient textures—charred allium, grain, herb, ceramic glaze—without becoming a generic food montage.
- **1.5–3.1:** Heat currents gather ingredients into a circular menu field arranged by intensity/temperature rather than conventional categories.
- **3.1–4.8:** A cursor/focus path follows one ingredient to its sourcing story. Exact location or producer names appear only if truthful; concept version uses category-level language.
- **4.8–6.4:** Dietary and accessibility filters alter the field without hiding the whole experience: vegetarian, allergen conversation, lower-sensory seating/access information. No medical safety guarantee.
- **6.4–8.1:** Heat line divides into TABLE / PRIVATE EVENT. The reservation route asks party size and preference but shows no fake availability.
- **8.1–10.0:** Menu field collapses into one ember-red ring/mark. It lands on the exact live sensory menu entrance, then cools to the opening coal for the loop.

**Open-site handoff:** the focused ingredient remains active in live HTML; users can immediately filter, inspect sourcing, or enter reservation/private-event flow.

**Avoid:** “Supper Club” framing, generic chef flames, fake sourcing claims, fake dietary guarantees, reservation dates without real inventory.

## Production sequence

1. Approve each project’s rebuilt identity and defining live interaction.
2. Capture the exact live entrance keyframe.
3. Produce one generated/rendered material-motion plate per project.
4. Composite fixed identity, exact interface copy, diagrams, and truthful states manually.
5. Build the final-frame-to-HTML handoff.
6. Export 10-second H.264/WebM review masters and posters.
7. Test at actual wheel-card size before approval.
8. Integrate one film at a time; never replace an existing film with an inferior benchmark.
